Chapter two

An outsourced commercial department, on retainer.

Three things, in order. Each one ends in a document you can hold.

Value it

We audit everything you can sell. Shirt, sleeve, back of shirt, training kit, LED, matchday, digital, community, data. Then we price it against what comparable rights actually cleared this season, not against what you charged last season and rolled over.

Deliverable: inventory audit and a priced media pack.

Sell it

We take the packages to market by category, not by mailing list. A named target list, brands with a reason to be in front of your audience, approached in the right order. You see every conversation. You sign every deal.

Deliverable: named target list by category, and the deals.

Prove it

Then the part nobody does. Every quarter, the sponsor gets a report they will actually read: logo screen time in minutes, reach by asset, what it was worth against what they paid, and a straight recommendation on renewal. Deals die at year one because nobody ever showed the sponsor what they got.

Deliverable: quarterly partner report and a renewal case.

Chapter three

This is why the second year happens.

A sponsor who cannot measure what they bought will not buy it twice. Most clubs below the elite tier send nothing after the season ends, so renewal becomes a conversation about goodwill and budget, which is a conversation you lose.

We send this instead. Screen time counted, reach broken out by asset, cost per thousand against what they paid, and a recommendation we are prepared to defend. When the number says renew at a higher rate, it says so. When it says the LED package underdelivered, it says that too, and we fix it before the sponsor raises it.

Chapter four

We are not right for every club.

This is for

  • Women's Super League 2 and tier 3 clubs with an audience growing faster than the commercial team can sell it.
  • Independent women's clubs operating outside a men's parent structure.
  • Women's teams inside a men's parent club who are being sold as an afterthought in a bundled deal.
  • Clubs who have never had their inventory valued by anyone who does this for a living.

This is not for

  • Clubs with a full commercial department already selling at market rate.
  • Anyone looking for a grant, a partner, or a cause.
  • Clubs who want a logo on a bib in exchange for exposure.
  • Anyone who wants the deal announced before the reporting is built.
