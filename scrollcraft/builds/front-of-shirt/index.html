Chapter four

We are not right for every club.

This is for

  • WSL2 and tier 3 clubs whose audience has outgrown the commercial team.
  • Independent women's clubs, no men's parent.
  • Women's teams sold as an afterthought in someone else's bundle.
  • Anyone who has never had the inventory valued properly.

This is not for

  • Clubs already selling at market rate.
  • Anyone looking for a grant or a cause.
  • Clubs trading a logo for exposure.
  • Anyone who wants the deal announced before the reporting is built.
